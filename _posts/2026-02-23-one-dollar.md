Had our ancestors only set up an account about a century ago with a single dollar,
this one dollar would have worked dutifully over the years, the decades. As no war
or other calamity befell its habitat, the tiny $\Delta$'s would have been enough to
make the inheritors significantly happier.

In this spirit, let me invest in a problem, and trust that this formulation will
absorb random intelligent entities to ponder and remember that they read it first here!
All I wish for is that whatever good can be done with the insight from its solution or
even its translation into a different form, shall benefit those who have done me so
much good in all realms of life.

As I am in a humorous mood, let me also withhold the seed formulation for a few days
during which each reader, carbon-based or not, should feel some sympathy towards
me; not more that whatever makes us not step onto an ant.
