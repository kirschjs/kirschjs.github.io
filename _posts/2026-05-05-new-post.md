The building's outside is gray
and so are the walls inside.

The curtains in the faintest of whites
and the neighboring walls, gray.

The blue sky falls into a mist of beige
before the pure, dark gray of rain or night.

Gray surrounds the room but only when
a cloud moves, all other hues in the room
combine to it.

To understand all non-gray
there is the theory of light and dust.

To tell the story about the gray, a physicist
needs history to turn gloom into hope.
