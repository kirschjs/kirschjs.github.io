Disregarding for this moment *global* consistency problems of the type that all presumed
*light* mass and energy cannot account for the universe' dynamics, I want to focus on
those experiments which we typically address as **scattering problems**. This limits
our view on those experiments which define/presume a certain initial and final state
and thereby infer laws about the interaction between the objects which constitute both.
