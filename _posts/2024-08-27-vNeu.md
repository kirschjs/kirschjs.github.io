In 1929, J. von Neumann and E. P. Wigner showed that states with point eigenvalues
$E\geq0$ could, nevertheless, be square integrable, i.e., would not resemble
systems like an electron moving away from a center to infinity. In contrast, the
authors provide an intuitive, classical interpretation of states which belong to
non-normalizable states which are **not** elements of the continuous spectrum but
obtain discrete eigenvalues when they reach infinity in finite time. For the complete
localization, they conjecture about an intriguing interference effect, and it is
the aim of this project to assist those to giants with the proof of their hypothesis.
